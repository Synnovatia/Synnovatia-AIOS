New Reverse Crunch (new, hip watch)
On your back, knees bent at 90 degrees, hands at your sides. Curl your hips a few inches off the floor using your abs, then lower over 3 seconds. Small and slow, no swinging. If the left hip complains, switch to Heel Taps: same position, lower back pressed down, tap one heel to the floor at a time.		 2 × 10–12 bodyweight 30 sec
