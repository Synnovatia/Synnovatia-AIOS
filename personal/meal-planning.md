## Recipe Rotation (added 2026-09-02)

> Nine simple, quick-cooking recipes following the Mayo Clinic diet approach above (whole foods, high protein, unlimited vegetables/fruit, minimal processed ingredients). All recipes serve 2 (Jackie + Leon). Full printable version with a consolidated shopping list sent separately.

### Breakfasts

**1. Veggie & Egg Scramble with Whole-Grain Toast** (~15 min)
4 eggs; 1 cup baby spinach, chopped; 1/2 bell pepper, diced; 2 tbsp diced onion; 1 tsp olive oil; salt, pepper, pinch garlic powder; 2 slices whole-grain bread; 1 cup fresh berries or sliced papaya.
Heat oil in a nonstick pan over medium. Sauté onion and pepper 3–4 min until softened, add spinach, cook until just wilted (1 min). Whisk eggs with salt/pepper, pour in, gently fold until just set (2–3 min) — don't overcook. Toast bread while eggs finish. Serve with fruit.

**2. Greek Yogurt Oat Parfait** (~5 min, no-cook)
1 1/2 cups plain nonfat or low-fat Greek yogurt; 1/2 cup old-fashioned rolled oats; 1 cup mixed berries; 2 tbsp chopped walnuts; optional dash of cinnamon.
Layer yogurt, oats, and berries in two glasses or bowls (oats soften as they sit — also works made the night before). Top with walnuts and cinnamon.

**3. Soft-Boiled Egg, Avocado Toast & Papaya** (~10 min)
2 eggs; 1/4 avocado, mashed; 2 slices whole-grain bread; squeeze of lemon, salt, pepper, red pepper flakes (optional); 1 cup sliced papaya.
Boil water, gently lower in eggs, boil 6–7 min for a soft-set yolk, transfer to ice water for a minute, peel. Toast bread, mash avocado with lemon/salt/pepper, spread on toast, top with sliced or halved egg. Serve papaya alongside.

### Lunches

**1. Mediterranean Chopped Salad with Grilled Chicken** (~20 min)
2 cups chopped romaine; 1 cucumber, diced; 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved; 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced; 1/4 cup crumbled feta; 1/4 cup kalamata olives; 2 grilled chicken breasts, sliced; dressing: 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp lemon juice, oregano, salt, pepper.
Season and grill or pan-sear chicken 5–6 min per side until 165°F internal, rest 5 min, slice. Toss salad ingredients with dressing, top with chicken.

**2. Turkey & Veggie Wrap with Side Salad** (~10 min)
1 whole-wheat tortilla per person; 4–6 slices deli turkey; 1 slice reduced-fat cheese (optional); handful spinach or mixed greens; sliced cucumber, shredded carrot, sliced tomato; 1 tsp mustard or light mayo; side of 2 cups mixed greens with vinaigrette.
Spread mustard on wrap, layer turkey, cheese, and vegetables, roll tightly, slice in half. Serve with dressed side salad.

**3. Tuna & White Bean Salad over Greens** (~10 min, no-cook)
2 cans (5 oz each) tuna in water, drained; 1 can (15 oz) white beans, rinsed and drained; 1/2 cup diced celery; 2 tbsp diced red onion; 2 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1/2 lemon; salt, pepper, fresh parsley (optional); 4 cups mixed greens.
Combine tuna, beans, celery, onion. Whisk oil, lemon, salt, pepper, toss gently. Serve over greens.

### Dinners

**1. Sheet-Pan Baked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables** (~25 min — fish night)
2 salmon fillets (5–6 oz each); 2 cups broccoli florets; 1 zucchini, sliced; 1 cup cherry tomatoes; 2 tbsp olive oil, divided; salt, pepper, garlic powder, lemon wedges.
Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss vegetables with 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper on a sheet pan, roast 10 min. Push to the sides, add salmon (skin-down) brushed with remaining oil and seasoned, to the center. Roast 10–12 more min until salmon flakes easily. Finish with lemon.

**2. Herb-Grilled Chicken Breast with Sautéed Greens and Quinoa** (~25 min)
2 boneless chicken breasts; 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp each dried thyme/rosemary, salt, pepper; 3/4 cup quinoa (dry); 4 cups Swiss chard or kale, stems removed; 1 clove garlic, minced; 1 tsp olive oil for greens.
Start quinoa cooking (~15 min). Season and grill or pan-sear chicken 6–7 min per side until 165°F, rest 5 min, slice. Sauté garlic in oil 30 sec, add greens, cook until wilted (3–4 min). Serve chicken over quinoa with greens.

**3. Pork Chops with Roasted Apples and Sweet Potato** (~25 min)
2 boneless pork chops; 1 apple, sliced; 1 medium sweet potato, cubed; 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cinnamon (for apples), salt, pepper, thyme; 1 tbsp olive oil for chops.
Preheat oven to 400°F. Toss sweet potato with oil, salt, pepper, roast 15 min. Add apple slices tossed with cinnamon, roast 10 more min. Season pork chops with salt, pepper, thyme, sear in a hot skillet 4–5 min per side until 145°F internal, rest 5 min. Serve over roasted apples and sweet potato.
