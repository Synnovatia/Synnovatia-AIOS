Breakfast and Lunch Ideas

Simple, 10 minutes or less, each about 25–35 g protein. Goal: about 30 g at every meal.

MealWhat's in itProtein

Make it easy

  • Boil 8–10 eggs on Sunday for the week.
  • Buy a rotisserie chicken or cook extra chicken at dinner for lunches.
  • Mix two jars of overnight oats at once.
  • Wash and chop salad vegetables once, store in a container.

Keep on hand

  • Eggs, plain Greek yogurt, cottage cheese
  • Turkey slices, canned tuna, chicken
  • Oats, whole-grain bread, tortillas
  • Salad greens, vegetables, berries, papaya
  • Canned white beans, chickpeas, walnuts

Protein amounts are approximate, per person.
