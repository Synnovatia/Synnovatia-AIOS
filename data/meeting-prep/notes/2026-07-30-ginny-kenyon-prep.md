## Who's Most Likely to Buy

**Her own existing manual customers — not a new/cold audience.** They're already:
- Agency owners/DONs/staff educators managing Medicare-certified or compliance-driven agencies (self-selected as her ICP by having bought a manual)
- Already trust Kenyon HCC and have a purchase relationship on file
- Sitting on exactly the pain points CDU solves: readmission penalties, OASIS documentation accuracy, staff turnover/confidence, mandatory CE hours
- Zero-cost to reach — no new customer acquisition needed, just a warm-list campaign

Tightest-fit sub-segment: **Medicare Home Health & Hospice manual buyers** specifically, since readmissions and OASIS scoring directly affect their reimbursement — the ROI story is most concrete for them. Past webinar attendees are a secondary warm list.

## Strategies to Discuss

1. **Email the existing manual customer list.** Highest-ROI, lowest-cost move available. Lead with the operational problems (readmissions, OASIS accuracy, turnover, CE compliance), not "we have courses." Segment Medicare Home Health/Hospice buyers for the sharpest pitch.
2. **Bundle at point of sale.** Offer a relevant CDU course (or the All-Courses Package) as a checkout add-on for manual purchases — same pattern she already uses for "Consulting Time for Customizing Your Manual." Pitch: buy the policy, train the staff who'll follow it.
3. **Turn existing blog traffic into a funnel.** Her chronic-disease blog posts already exist and presumably get traffic — none currently link to the paid courses. Adding a direct "Enroll your staff" CTA to each is free conversion sitting on the table.
4. **Reframe pricing as compliance ROI, not training expense.** Agencies budget for compliance, not "nice to have" education. A simple one-pager — cost of one readmission/audit finding vs. cost of training — makes $150/seat read as risk reduction.
5. **Free sample module as a lead magnet.** Self-paced courses are a hard sell sight-unseen. One free module (Diabetes or CHF — her most universally relevant conditions) lowers the barrier and captures leads outside her existing list.
6. **State association / accreditation-body partnerships.** CDU is WA-certified and may satisfy other states — worth exploring bulk-licensing or referral deals with state home care associations, especially given she already serves the "Accredited Manuals" segment.
7. **Move toward recurring revenue.** Currently one-time per-seat. An annual all-staff-plus-new-hires license (especially attractive to higher-turnover agencies) would convert occasional purchases into the passive income she originally wanted from CDU.

## Suggested Agenda

Given she has no active marketing motion today, start with #1 (email to existing list) as the immediate next step — it requires no new asset creation and reaches the warmest possible audience first. Bundle-at-checkout (#2) and the blog CTA fix (#3) are the next-cheapest wins if she wants to keep momentum after that.
