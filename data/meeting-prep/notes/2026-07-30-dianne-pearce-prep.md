## The Book

Three-section memoir — her grandmother Marta Forgaard, her mother Lillian Robinson, and herself — framed around a "warrior" theme (surviving divorce, bankruptcy, building a business as a self-made millionaire). Written as a legacy for her children, Christopher and JoAnne. Intended to inspire women broadly, with humor mixed into the hard parts.

## Goals & Intentions (her own words, from the coaching-questions doc)

- **Purpose:** preserve real family history "so we will not become invisible," and offer other women hope, resilience, and laughs through the hard parts
- **Timeline:** 2 years max
- **Structure:** No rigid chapter plan — she wants a "flow," which she calls a "design-build" project
- **Her stated sequence:** (1) big-picture plan/strategy for the whole book, (2) section formatting, (3) content/editing within each story, (4) then decide: pitch publishers or self-publish
- **Done = ?** Everything edited to her satisfaction (including Jackie's feedback and a few others') and either a publisher lined up or a self-publishing plan in place
- **What she wants from the coaching relationship:** keep her focused, feedback on both content and writing style, "amazing questions," monthly calls (+ a short second call if needed) — and explicitly wants **encouragement first, critique second**, plus clarity on next steps after each call
- **What blocks her:** no clear plan/structure, indecision on first- vs. third-person voice, and — her words — "happy hours, card games, tee-off times, bikes to ride, and nap time." Her go-to unstick strategy is talking it through with Jackie or "Doug."

## Manuscript Status — Where the Actual Work Has Gone (as of 2026-07-29)

Her stated sequence says plan-first, then content. The drafts in Drive show she's actually already well past that, unevenly, across the three sections:

- **Marta's section (grandmother) — most developed.** Four substantial first-person narrative chapters: immigrating from Norway as a young child after her mother's death, an abusive father, and running away at 16 after a violent incident. **Contains a disclosed passage of childhood sexual abuse by the father** — heavy material, worth being ready for emotionally. Chapters 5–8 are headers only, no content yet. She's left herself open research questions inline (e.g. "Dianne — find out what she died of?", "Finish this chapter Dianne").
- **Lillian's section (mother) — underway, lighter in tone so far.** Chapter 1 is developed: born 1919 in Saskatoon, a strict-but-loving mother (Marta), standing up to a teacher's false cheating accusation. Contrasts with Marta's darker material.
- **Dianne's own section — least developed.** Not drafted as narrative at all yet — just a long, unsorted list of story fragments and one-liners spanning her life: first dates, an appendix scare in Italy at 19, birth of her kids, the collapse of her first marriage (financial betrayal, bankruptcy, single parenting two young kids), life with Doug, Mexico misadventures, lake life. Raw material, not yet shaped into scenes.

**The real starting point for tomorrow isn't "she hasn't picked a plan yet"** — it's that content generation is already ahead of structure, unevenly across sections, and the most emotionally weighted material (Marta) is also the most drafted. Worth naming this gap directly rather than defaulting to the old "start with big-picture plan" agenda, since the ground has shifted since July 26.
