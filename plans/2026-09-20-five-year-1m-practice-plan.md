# Five-Year Plan: $1M/Year Solo Coaching Practice

> Written 2026-09-20. Long-horizon plan Jackie asked for — grow Synnovatia to $1M/year in strategy consulting for small businesses within 5 years (by ~Sep 2031). Treats the existing near-term strategy in `context/strategy.md` (the ~$100K/yr target by early 2027) as **Year 1 of this plan**, not a separate initiative — everything currently active feeds into this.

---

## The Real Math First

Before a timeline: **$1M/year cannot come from Jackie's own 1:1 delivery time alone, at any price.** Worth saying plainly rather than assuming it away.

Current capacity is ~3 hrs/day on Synnovatia (`context/personal-info.md`) — call it ~15 hrs/week, ~750 hrs/year. Even fully booked with top-tier retainer clients ($1,100/mo, 4 meetings) at a 40% rate increase ($1,540/mo = $18,480/yr each), and assuming each client realistically costs ~2 hrs/week of session + prep + follow-up time, the honest ceiling is **6-8 concurrent retainer clients** — roughly $110K-$150K/yr from 1:1 work, full stop. Add both masterminds at full capacity (Seven Figure Forum: $1,299/round × 2 rounds × up to 8 members ≈ $20,800/yr per room; Mastermind for the Messy Middle: $675/qtr × 4 × 6 paying seats ≈ $16,200/yr per room) and the realistic solo ceiling — one person, current capacity, every seat full, prices already raised 40% — lands around **$180K-$220K/yr**. That's a strong outcome and roughly double the current active goal, but it's not $1M, and no amount of "work harder" closes a 4-5x gap against a fixed-hours constraint.

**This means "$1M in 5 years" and "solo" are in real tension**, and the plan below resolves it the way Jackie's own stated long-term vision already points: stay the face, brand, and strategic authority of Synnovatia — but stop being the only delivery mechanism. `context/strategy.md`'s existing Longer-Term Vision already names this ("bring in another consultant to serve the $250K–$500K segment, freeing Jackie to focus on higher-tier clients and business development"). This plan treats that as load-bearing, not optional, and builds the timeline around it.

**The alternative — a genuinely one-person practice — tops out around $200K/yr even at full capacity and premium pricing.** If "$1M" is a hard requirement, leverage beyond Jackie's own hours is the only path there from where the business stands today. If "solo" is the harder requirement, $1M isn't the right target number — that's worth deciding explicitly, not discovering three years in. See Open Questions.

---

## Assumptions This Plan Makes (flag if any are wrong)

1. Jackie stays the sole *strategic* voice/brand — but by Year 2-3, an associate consultant delivers day-to-day work for the $250K-$500K segment under the Synnovatia name, per the existing long-term vision.
2. The 40% rate increase (already active, target Jan 2027) lands and holds without major client attrition.
3. School (~20 hrs/week through ~Oct 2026) doesn't expand into a multi-year commitment that further caps the ~3 hrs/day business capacity — if it does, every timeline below shifts right.
4. Mastermind pricing and room caps stay roughly as structured today ($675/qtr MMM, $1,299/round SFF, 6-8 seats/room), with room *count* growing rather than a fundamental repricing — except where Year 4-5 explicitly assumes a premium-tier repricing once the brand is established.
5. No outside capital — matches the brand's own bootstrapped positioning; growth is funded from operating revenue.

## Year-by-Year

### Year 1 (now – ~Q1 2027): Finish what's already active

This is `context/strategy.md`'s current plan, unchanged — this document doesn't replace it, it builds on top of it.

- 5 new retainer clients (by ~Oct 2026)
- Seven Figure Forum to 6 members (by Jan 2027); Mastermind for the Messy Middle to 8 total/6 paying (by Oct 2026), second MMM cohort launching Jan 2027
- 40% rate increase live (by ~Jan 2027), tracked against the `context/brand-positioning-metrics.md` baseline (0% progress as of the 2026-09-13 baseline)
- Website/brand relaunch already shipped (2026-09-11) — this year is about proving it converts, not building more of it
- **Target run rate exiting Year 1: ~$100K/yr** (the existing goal)

**What Year 1 needs to prove before Year 2 can start:** that the repositioned brand supports premium pricing without losing clients (watch the retention/pushback tracking already set up in `brand-positioning-metrics.md`), and that demand exists beyond what Jackie alone can serve — i.e., real evidence the $250K-$500K segment has more people wanting in than she has hours for. That evidence is the actual go/no-go signal for hiring an associate, not a calendar date.

### Year 2 (~2027-2028): Add leverage — the associate consultant

- Bring on one associate consultant to deliver 1:1/retainer work for the $250K-$500K segment (the segment Jackie's own long-term vision already earmarks for this). Jackie shifts toward: strategic oversight of associate-delivered work, the $1M+ Seven Figure Forum segment, and business development.
- Grow Seven Figure Forum to a second room (2 rooms × up to 8 members) — this is Jackie's own highest-leverage-per-hour offer (group format, premium clients) and doesn't require the associate.
- **Target run rate exiting Year 2: ~$220K-$260K/yr** — roughly: Jackie's own reduced-but-premium 1:1/VIP load + 2 SFF rooms + 2 MMM rooms + the associate's first full year of retainer delivery (ramping, not yet full).

**Real risk to flag:** finding and onboarding the right associate is a relationship/trust problem, not a hiring-funnel problem, given how much of Synnovatia's value is "peer-level relational depth" (`brand-voice.md`). This will likely take longer than a clean calendar quarter — build in slack, don't force it to a date.

### Year 3 (~2028-2029): Scale what's working, add a second leverage line

- Associate consultant fully ramped (a full book of $250K-$500K retainer clients)
- Consider a second associate, or the first associate taking on some Seven Figure Forum-adjacent 1:1 work, depending on where actual demand is by this point
- Introduce one scalable/productized offer — something that doesn't cost Jackie or an associate linear hours per client. Given the brand's actual strength (25+ years of pattern-recognition, published author, a real methodology), the natural candidate is a cohort-based program or a licensed/structured version of the "Messy Middle" diagnostic work — built from what's already proven in 1:1 and mastermind delivery, not invented from scratch. This needs its own dedicated planning pass closer to the time; noted here as a placeholder line, not designed yet.
- **Target run rate exiting Year 3: ~$420K-$480K/yr**

### Year 4 (~2029-2030): Premium repositioning at the top

- By now the brand has multiple years of proof (case studies, retention data, the "Strategic Perspective" positioning fully lived-in) — this is the point to raise Seven Figure Forum and top-tier 1:1 pricing again, not just the one-time 40% move from Year 1. The $1M+ segment is the least price-sensitive part of the business and the one most aligned with "Fortune 500-grade strategic expertise" positioning.
- Productized offer (started Year 3) should be generating real, largely-passive revenue by now if it's working — if it isn't, this is the checkpoint to kill it rather than keep funding it.
- **Target run rate exiting Year 4: ~$680K-$750K/yr**

### Year 5 (~2030-2031): Close the gap to $1M

- At this point the plan is mostly execution, not new-initiative-building: full associate capacity (possibly a second associate), 2-3 mastermind rooms per program at premium pricing, Jackie's own time concentrated entirely on the highest-value strategic/VIP work and the productized offer's ongoing growth.
- **Target: $1M/yr run rate**

*A rough Year-5 composition, order-of-magnitude only, not a forecast to hold anyone to:* Jackie's own premium VIP/strategic work (~$250-300K) + Seven Figure Forum at 2-3 rooms and higher pricing (~$60-90K) + Mastermind for the Messy Middle at 2-3 rooms (~$50-75K) + associate-delivered retainer work, possibly two associates by now (~$150-250K) + the productized offer (~$150-250K). These numbers are illustrative scaffolding to sanity-check the shape of the plan — the real mix gets decided in Years 2-4 based on what's actually working.

## What This Plan Deliberately Doesn't Do

- **It doesn't assume the associate-consultant model works on the first try.** If it doesn't — if the right person isn't found, or client trust doesn't transfer the way the brand's relational positioning depends on — this whole plan needs re-scoping, likely toward a lower, genuinely-solo ceiling (~$200K/yr) rather than forcing $1M through a broken mechanism.
- **It doesn't invent the Year 3 productized offer's actual design.** That's real, separate strategic work — a placeholder here, a dedicated plan later.
- **It doesn't assume school ends and stays ended.** If a new term or program starts after Oct 2026, capacity assumptions in every year shift.
