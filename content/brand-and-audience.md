## Brand Positioning

**Who you are:** Jackie, founder and sole practitioner of Synnovatia — a strategic consulting practice for bootstrapped B2B service business owners navigating the "Messy Middle" ($250K–$4M revenue).

**Your authority:** 25+ years of cross-industry pattern recognition working with hundreds of small businesses; educator background (Corporate Coach University, Coach University — taught small business development/marketing); Entrepreneurship certification from UCLA; runs her own business, so clients experience her as a peer, not an outside "expert."

**Brand voice:** Peer, not guru. Precise, not generic. Warm, not soft. Confident, not boastful. Equip, not help.

**Brand pillars:**
- **Human Peer Dynamic** — relates as a fellow business owner, not a detached expert (strongest cross-interview signal)
- **Warmth as Strategic Differentiator** — functional, not soft; most competitors can't manufacture it
- **Outside Objectivity** — gives clients the 30,000-ft view they're too close to their own business to see themselves
- **Practical Collaboration, Not Cheerleading** — rolls up her sleeves and delivers something practical, not just encouragement

## Target Audience Segments

### Segment 1: Messy Middle Owner ($250K–$500K)

- **Who they are:** Founder/CEO of a bootstrapped B2B service business, 5-10 person team, stalled growth, wearing too many hats
- **What they care about:** Getting unstuck, finding a peer group of similarly-staged owners, affordable strategic support
- **What they want:** To break through the plateau and grow revenue
- **What they need:** Outside objectivity, a structured peer environment, and permission to stop doing everything alone
- **Language level:** Plainspoken, no corporate jargon, name the exact revenue range so they self-identify
- **Where they are in the journey:** Often unaware a mastermind/advisor is what they need — meet them at "I feel stuck," not "I need a coach" (candidate for Mastermind for the Messy Middle)

### Segment 2: Scaling B2B Owner ($1M+)

- **Who they are:** Founder/CEO past the earliest stalls, running a $1M+ bootstrapped B2B service business, facing more complex strategic decisions
- **What they care about:** Peer-level strategic conversation with people at their revenue tier, sharpening decision-making
- **What they want:** To keep scaling without losing what makes the business work
- **What they need:** A mixed-gender peer group of similarly-scaled owners plus Fortune 500-caliber strategic input (candidate for Seven Figure Forum)
- **Language level:** Confident, strategic, precise — this audience has less patience for generic small-business content
- **Where they are in the journey:** Often already advisor-aware; the differentiator is proving Synnovatia's caliber, not introducing the concept of outside advice

### Segment 3: 1:1 / Retainer Prospect

- **Who they are:** Any Stage II owner ($250K–$4M) not ready for a group format — wants individual strategic attention
- **What they care about:** A direct relationship with one advisor who knows their specific business
- **What they want:** Ongoing strategic partnership, not a one-off consult
- **What they need:** Proof that Jackie will stay engaged between sessions, not disappear after the call ends
- **Language level:** Warm, specific, low-pressure — this segment converts through trust built over time, not urgency
- **Where they are in the journey:** Ranges from cold (LinkedIn/blog discovery) to warm (former client re-engagement, referral)
