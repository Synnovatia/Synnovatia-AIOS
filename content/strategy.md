## Competitive Positioning

**Your unique angle:** "Different Is Better Than Better" — Synnovatia doesn't compete by being better along the same dimensions as other consultants/coaches; it wins by sitting at the intersection of Fortune 500-grade strategic expertise and genuine peer-level relational depth. Jackie runs her own business, so clients experience her as a peer, not an outside "expert."

**Key competitors / peers in your space** (by voice share, as tracked by Jackie 2026-07-27):

| Competitor | Voice Share | Actual Target Market | ICP Fit ($250K–$4M bootstrapped B2B) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic Coach | 29% | $200K+ personal net income, 3+ yrs in business, $12–25K/yr membership | **Skews higher/more established** — price point alone screens out most of the Messy Middle segment |
| EOS Worldwide | 29% | 10–250 employees | **Skews larger** — Synnovatia's core clients (5–10 people) sit at EOS's floor, not its center of gravity |
| Vistage | 21% | $5M+ revenue required (niche groups down to $1M), 20+ employees, ~$19K/yr | **Poor fit for Messy Middle, closer to Seven Figure Forum tier** — but even $5M+ exceeds Synnovatia's $4M ceiling |
| ActionCoach | 14% | $250K–$5M revenue, 1–10 employees | **Close match** — nearly identical band to Synnovatia's core ICP |
| The Alternative Board (TAB) | ~2%* | ~30K members, avg. member revenue ~$975K, no hard floor | **Broad/mixed** — skews above Messy Middle on average |
| Focal Point (Brian Tracy) | ~2%* | No clear revenue floor found; franchise-coach model, ROI-multiplier pitch | **Unclear — likely broad, not Stage-II specific** |

*TAB and Focal Point were tracked as a combined 4% share; split evenly here pending a more precise breakdown.

**What the ICP check (2026-07-27 web research) actually found:** only ActionCoach is a genuine same-ICP competitor. Strategic Coach, EOS, Vistage, and TAB mostly compete for larger, more established businesses than the $250K–$500K Messy Middle band — Vistage and Strategic Coach in particular are priced and gated well above what a Messy Middle prospect would even consider (Vistage's own $5M floor exceeds Synnovatia's entire $4M ceiling). This sharpens the differentiation story: it isn't "Synnovatia vs. five equally-matched competitors" — it's that Synnovatia is one of very few players actually built for this specific stage, which is a stronger content angle than direct feature-for-feature comparison.

**How Synnovatia differentiates:** all five are methodology-first, franchise-model, or scale-driven — none combine Fortune 500-grade strategic depth with genuine peer-level relational warmth the way Synnovatia does (see "Genuine in a Market Full of Grifts" and "Human Peer Dynamic" proof points in brand-voice.md). Content can lean into this contrast without naming competitors directly — e.g. "most business coaching is a franchise playbook applied to your business" — per brand voice, confidence over comparison.

## Content Rules

- Voice follows `context/brand-voice.md` throughout: peer not guru, precise not generic, warm not soft, confident not boastful, equip not help (never use the word "help")
- Name the actual revenue range ($250K–$4M) and stage ("Messy Middle") rather than generic language like "small business owners"
- **Mandatory AI-tell scrub** before any draft is presented — no negation pivots ("It's not X. It's Y."), stacked punchy fragments, anaphoric repetition, "Here's the thing" openers, or formulaic triads
- **Mandatory blunter/plainer/warmer pass** before any draft is presented — cut corporate jargon (pivot, runway, leverage, optimize), cut throat-clearing/meta-explanation, default shorter, keep informal punctuation (exclamation points, rhetorical questions) as on-voice warmth
- Never name a client without confirming they're comfortable being named — default to anonymized/generalized
- No auto-posting, ever — every draft is reviewed by Jackie before it goes anywhere (LinkedIn or blog)

## Success Signals (Priority Scoring)

What counts as a "win," used to inform `priority_score` (1-10) during `/develop` and `/schedule`. All of the below matter — weight a piece higher when it plausibly drives more than one:

- **Engagement** (likes/comments/shares on LinkedIn) — signals what resonates and is worth developing further/repeating
- **Connection/follower growth** — attributable new connections or followers
- **Discovery calls booked** — direct attribution to booked 1:1 consultations (the clearest revenue signal for retainer-track content)
- **Mastermind applications** — direct attribution to Messy Middle or Seven Figure Forum applications (the clearest revenue signal for mastermind-track content)

No automated tracking exists yet for any of these — Jackie reports outcomes as she observes them (e.g. "that post got 3 discovery call requests"), and `/develop`/`/schedule` should ask when relevant rather than assume silence means a piece underperformed.
