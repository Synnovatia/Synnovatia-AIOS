## Funnels

### Primary Funnel (LinkedIn / Blog → Retainer or Mastermind)

```
LinkedIn post or Blog article (awareness)
    |
    v
Profile visit / connection request / website visit
    |
    v
Warm follow-up (LinkedIn DM or website booking CTA)
    |
    v
Discovery call — the actual conversion moment (per Mark Chapman's interview,
trust is earned here, not in marketing copy)
    |
    v
Retainer client OR mastermind application
```

### Re-engagement Funnel (Former Clients)

```
6-month re-engagement email (see client-reengagement/)
    |
    v
Reply / meeting scheduled
    |
    v
Renewed retainer OR mastermind invite
```

## Audience → Offer Alignment

| Audience Segment | Content Pillar | On-Content CTA | Offer Positioning |

| Messy Middle Owner | Thought leadership, Story | "Apply for Consideration" | Peer-group framing — you're not alone at this stage |
| Scaling B2B Owner | Thought leadership, Client win | "Apply for Consideration" | Caliber framing — Fortune 500-grade strategic peers |
| 1:1/Retainer Prospect | Story, Client win | "Schedule a Conversation" / "Start the Conversation" | Relationship framing — ongoing partnership, not a one-off |
| Cold/SEO (blog readers) | SEO/blog | CTA-light — no hard ask | Blog traffic is colder and less brand-aware than LinkedIn; let posts build authority and let the reader self-select into exploring the site rather than pitching directly |
