## Draft 1 — General version (What I'm Watching, not a hard CTA this cycle)

**Subject:** What I'm watching this week, and what's worth knowing for your business

**Preview text:** Cash flow just became small business owners' #1 worry, ahead of inflation, and buyers now expect a payback timeline before they'll sign.

Hi there,

In the 25+ years of working with service-based businesses, there's never been a more upside-down time than what we're experiencing now. So, to help you navigate these uncertain times, here are a few things I'm keeping my eye on…

**Cash flow** remains an owners' top concern, even as optimism keeps climbing. If a prospect seems hesitant lately, it may be less about whether they believe in the work and [more about wanting to see a clear, fast payback before committing](https://www.nfib.com/news/press-release/new-nfib-survey-small-business-optimism-picks-up-in-june/). How do you communicate the return realistically to set expectations? It's something to think about during your sales calls.

This aligns with a broader shift in **how B2B buyers evaluate a purchase.** A recent buyer-behavior study found most [buyers now expect an ROI within three months](https://sell.g2.com/2026-buyer-behavior-report). Building a payback timeline into a proposal matters more than leading with value alone.

**Growth in 2026** for service businesses is coming less from publishing more content and more from [removing a buyer's doubt before they ask](https://www.fastcompany.com/91502717/how-service-businesses-can-grow-in-2026). A few client results with real numbers, pricing transparency, and a referral network that keeps sending you work, all do more than another piece of thought leadership. (Thank goodness! We're all choking on content these days.)

That's what's on my radar for now. If any of this is useful, or you want to talk through how it applies to your business, hit reply and let me know.

Until next time...

Jackie
