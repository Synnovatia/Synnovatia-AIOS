## "What I'm Watching" — Drifting Edition (FINAL)

**Subject:** What I'm watching. What's worth knowing for your business right now?

**Preview text:** Owners are more optimistic but still holding back on spending, AI's impact on business value, and a few other things worth knowing.

Hi there,

There are a few things I'm keeping an eye on for anyone running a service business in the $250K–$4M range:

Owner confidence is up, but spending isn't following yet. The National Federation of Independent Business (NFIB) — the trade group that tracks small business sentiment — [just clocked its highest reading since February](https://www.nfib.com/news/press-release/new-nfib-survey-small-business-optimism-picks-up-in-june/). Roughly two-thirds of owners [expect revenue growth this year](https://www.uschamber.com/small-business/small-business-index-q2-2026). (Hope that's you!) Nearly half of small businesses are building up cash reserves instead of expanding. If that's you too, that instinct is probably right.

You may not be financing growth with debt however you may be interested in knowing that [interest rates are likely to hold steady this week](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/fed-interest-rate-decision-july-meeting/) which may impact your work with your clients. Rates are also likely to stay high into the fall. If you're bootstrapped like most of the owners I work with, your own cash reserves are doing the job borrowing would do for someone else. It's worth keeping that muscle strong regardless of what the Fed does next.

If you're thinking about selling your business someday — business buyers are increasingly paying for systems, not just revenue. [M&A activity in professional services picked up 13% this quarter](https://www.cbh.com/insights/articles/professional-services-industry-outlook-trends/). Private equity firms are targeting businesses with standardized processes and repeatable delivery.

And if you're building those systems, AI is becoming a part of how you can achieve that. [Most small business owners — 76% — are already using AI in some form](https://www.goldmansachs.com/pressroom/press-releases/2026/small-businesses-embrace-ai-but-need-training-and-support-to-fully-harness-it), and 93% say it's made a real difference. But only 14% have actually built it into how the business runs day to day. (BTW, I'm in the process of doing this!) Incorporating AI into your actual operations builds a documented, repeatable business — one that's more valuable to run today, and more valuable to sell down the road.

That's all for now.

If you found this information helpful, I'd appreciate it if you would hit reply and let me know what you found most helpful.

Until next time...

Jackie
