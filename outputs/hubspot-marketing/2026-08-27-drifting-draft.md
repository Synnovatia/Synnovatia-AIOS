## "What I'm Watching" — Drifting Edition (FINAL)

**Subject:** What I'm Watching: Rates, Hiring, and Where AI Is Actually Landing

**Preview text:** A few things worth keeping an eye on if you're running a B2B service business right now.

Hi there,

A few things I've been paying attention to lately for the B2B service business sector:

**Borrowing costs** have stayed elevated for a while now. That's shaping decisions even for businesses that aren't carrying much debt themselves. If you're bootstrapped you're probably not financing growth but your clients might be. A tighter lending environment makes buyers more cautious and deliberate before they commit to anything new. Worth factoring into pricing and timing of your own offers right now.

**Hiring** is still one of the harder parts of running a service business. Finding someone who fits takes longer than it used to. Owners I talk to are choosing to stay lean rather than rush a hire they might regret. If that's you, that instinct is probably the right one. A bad hire almost always costs more than staying short-staffed a little longer.

**Client budgets** are still tight in a lot of industries. The businesses winning right now are the ones making the value of their work obvious. If you haven't looked recently at how clearly your pricing connects to the outcome you deliver, that's worth a second look.

And **AI** keeps showing up in more of how businesses operate though the gap between "using it" and "actually building it into how the business runs" is still wide for most. *I'm in the middle of closing that gap in my own business. It's slower than the headlines make it sound although it is making a difference in how much time certain tasks are taking.* If you're in the same spot, welcome to the club.

That's what's on my radar this month.

If any of this is useful, I'd love to hear what's resonating. Hit reply and let me know. And, let me know what you're watching.

Until next time...

Jackie
