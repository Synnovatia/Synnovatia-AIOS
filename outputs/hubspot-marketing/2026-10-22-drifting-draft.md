## Earlier version (not used): What I'm Watching

**Subject:** Why so many decisions are stalling right now

**Preview text:** A few things on my radar this month for B2B service businesses.

Hi there,

Here are a few things on my radar this month for B2B service businesses:

**Uncertainty** is running high. NFIB's Uncertainty Index reached 90 in September, well above its long-run average of 68 ([NFIB](https://www.nfib.com/news/press-release/new-nfib-survey-uncertainty-grows-and-optimism-fades-slightly-on-main-street-in-september/)). When owners can't see what's coming, they wait. For a service business, that may look like a proposal that sits or a start date that keeps sliding. Try this: go back to one prospect who went quiet and offer a smaller first step they can say yes to now.

**Stalled deals** are often stuck on the buyer's side. In RAIN Group's latest research, 79% said losing deals to "no decision" has stayed the same or gotten worse ([RAIN Group](https://www.rainsalestraining.com/blog/no-decision-still-blocks-revenue)). The person you spoke with usually has to convince someone else, in a meeting you won't be in. Try this: send your contact a short summary they can forward, covering the problem, the cost of waiting, and what you recommend.

**AI research** is now part of how buyers find you. Among buyers who used AI to research vendors, 94% still checked the answers somewhere else ([TrustRadius](https://research.agilebrandguide.com/research/trustradius-bridging-the-b2b-buying-disconnect-ai-trust-and-go-to-market-strategies-for-2026)). That somewhere else is often your website and LinkedIn. Try this: ask ChatGPT to recommend someone who does what you do, for the kind of clients you serve, and read how it describes you, or whether you show up at all.

**Hiring** is still hard. Owners named labor quality as their top problem in NFIB's September survey, for the third month in a row. Try this: before you post your next role, ask your best current employee who they would want working next to them.

That's what I'm watching these days. What are you seeing in your business?

Until next time...

Jackie

> Stats in this version are unverified (from the 10/15-10/21 What I'm Watching research emails): NFIB uncertainty and labor quality, RAIN Group 79%, TrustRadius 94% via a third-party summary.
