## "What I'm Watching" — Lapsed Edition

**Subject:** What I'm Watching

**Preview text:** Owner confidence is climbing, profitability's turning a corner, and what the highest-growth firms are doing differently with their marketing budget.

Hi there,

A few things I've been keeping an eye on for anyone running a service business in the $250K–$4M range.

Owner confidence jumped last month. The share of small business CEOs who think the economy is worse than a year ago fell to 34%, down from 48% in June, according to the latest Vistage/WSJ survey of owners at $1M-$20M companies. That's a big one-month swing, worth watching rather than treating as a settled trend, but the direction is real.

The numbers back it up. Bank of America's account data shows small business profitability turned positive in June for the first time this year, and hiring activity is picking back up from where it was earlier in 2026. If cash flow has felt tight for you or your clients, that squeeze may finally be starting to ease.

Here's a number worth checking yourself against. Hinge Marketing's newest study of 495 professional services firms found high-growth firms are putting 12% of revenue into marketing, versus just 5% for firms that aren't growing, and growing roughly four times faster as a result.

One more, on AI. 95% of B2B marketers say they're already using it, but only about 39% say it's actually moving the needle on performance, per Content Marketing Institute's latest research. That gap usually comes down to whether the fundamentals underneath the tool are solid, not the tool itself.

Until next time...

Jackie
