## Monday, August 24 — Thought Leadership

When you're overwhelmed, the advice is always the same: do a time audit. Track every 15 minutes for a week and see where it's going. Frankly, I've tried it and it doesn't really change my pattern. It tells me what I already know.

What IS working for me is deciding how I want to spend my time each day. Plus, I feel less frustrated because my priorities are continually getting sidelined.

If you have a technique that's working for you, I'd love to hear it.

## Friday, August 28 — Client Win

I listened to a podcast episode on agile/scrum this week (Agile-Driven Impact). Fascinating, even though I'm a total novice on the subject.

The question that got me: how do you actually define "done" when you're scoping a project?

Listening to it, I realized I do this half-consciously all the time. I start defining "done," then drift off mid-definition without ever finishing the thought.

I tested it for real on my current project, redesigning my own website. Forcing myself to fully answer "what does done look like" before starting gave me a far more complete, strategic plan than I would have landed on otherwise.

One simple question, worth asking before a project starts, not halfway through it.
