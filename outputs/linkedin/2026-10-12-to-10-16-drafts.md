## Monday, October 12 — Thought Leadership

Have you ever compared what your software can do with what you actually use it for?

I see this a lot in businesses between $250K and $400K. The tools are already paid for (a CRM, an invoicing system) and most are doing a fraction of what they could. (In case you're wondering, 30-40% of what the software can do typically goes unused.)

When growth stalls at this stage, we tend to go shopping for another tool or another hire. It's usually cheaper to start with what we already own.

What's one tool you'd say you're getting full use out of?
