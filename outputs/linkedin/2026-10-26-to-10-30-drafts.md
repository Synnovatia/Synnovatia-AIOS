## Monday, October 26 — Thought Leadership

Have you ever felt like you're the first person to hit a particular wall in your business – like it's only ever happened to you?

When owners tell me growth has stalled, it almost always sounds like a brand-new problem. From the inside, it feels that way. The walls tend to show up in the same places, though: somewhere between $250K and $400K, again around $750K to $1M, and again near $3M. The cause is different at each one, and that's good news, because other owners have already found their way through.

Did growth ever get noticeably harder for you at a particular revenue number?
