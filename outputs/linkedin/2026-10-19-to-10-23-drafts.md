## Monday, October 19 — Thought Leadership

Have you ever noticed that what brought in your first clients isn't bringing in the next ones?

I see this in businesses between $750K and $1M. Early on, we take whoever shows up and describe what we do broadly enough to fit all of them. (That's how most of us got started!) The trouble is that a broad description is hard for a prospect to remember and even harder for a client to pass along.

Growth at this stage usually asks us to get more specific about who we serve and what we do better than anyone else. Is that scary? Yes, because it feels like turning business away.

Which client would you love to have 10 more of?

## Wednesday, October 21 — Story

My redesigned website went live last month. (Finally!) A couple of days later I checked the traffic numbers and found 188 visits had landed on "page not found." Oy!

Most of those people were looking for old blog posts. When the blog moved to a new platform, the old links never got pointed to the new ones, and I had no idea.

It made me wonder what else we set up years ago and never went back to check… a proposal template, maybe, or the voicemail greeting.

Is there anything in your business you set up years ago and haven't looked at since?
