## Friday, September 18 — Ongoing Story Series, Part 1

Part 1: how I actually figured out what makes Synnovatia different (a story in installments)

Last year I got tired of guessing why people choose to work with me over anyone else, so I asked the people who already had.

I sat down with eight clients, one at a time, plus an outside marketing advisor who'd watched my business from a distance but never worked with me directly. A handful of open questions, no script. I wanted what they actually said, not what I hoped to hear.

The first surprise: nobody said the same thing. Not one theme showed up in a majority of those conversations. If I'd been hoping for one clean, quotable answer, I didn't get it.

What I got instead took a lot longer to piece together, and it changed how I talk about this business more than I expected. More on that next week.

What would you actually hear if you asked your best clients why they picked you, instead of guessing?
