Different Stages of Business Growth Call for Different Kinds of Support

Some problems need an ongoing thinking partner. Others need one sharp conversation and a clear next step. Every engagement is built around your business and your aspirations.

Here's what each one actually looks like.

Strategic Coaching

An ongoing thinking partner for the stretch you're in now.

This is the deepest way to work together: a one-on-one engagement focused entirely on your business. We set the strategic direction, work the plan, and adjust as reality unfolds. It's individual attention, not a group format.

It's also where the work goes beyond strategy. Stage II ownership is isolating, and decisions carry weight you can't share with your team. The emotional reality of ownership is part of the strategic reality, not separate from it.

We meet on a cadence that matches what you're working through, whether that's once a month or closer to weekly. What doesn't change is the depth. This isn't a status check-in. You stay on my mind after our conversation ends.

Solutions on the Fly

We get to the core issue fast, pressure-test your options, and you leave with a decision you can act on.
