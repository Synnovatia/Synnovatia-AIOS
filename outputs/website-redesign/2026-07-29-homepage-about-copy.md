### The Pattern

**Header:** The Same Wall, More Than Once

Most owners hit the first one somewhere around $250,000–$400,000, when whatever got the business off the ground — hustle, doing everything yourself, figuring it out as you go — stops being enough. The second usually shows up around $750,000 to $1 million, once the business has outgrown what one person can run alone but hasn't built real structure yet. The third tends to hit around $3–4 million, when the business gets too complex to hold in your head the way you used to.

Each time, it rarely feels like part of a pattern. It feels like something's wrong — with the market, the team, or you. Usually, it's none of those things. The strategy that worked to get you here just has to change to get you through here.
