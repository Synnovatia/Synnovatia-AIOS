**H2:** The Mastermind for the Messy Middle

**Sub (italic):** A room built for exactly this stage, not the one before it or the one after.

This is a small, hand-selected group of women running B2B service businesses in the same $250K–$500K range, working through the same terrain at the same time. Four are already in the room.

We meet every other Friday morning and work through what's actually on the table: growing without burning out, who to hire and who to let go, the client conversation you've been putting off, using AI without losing what makes the business yours.

This isn't a networking group, and it isn't a cheerleading session. It's a room where the right minds work your actual problem until the path forward is clear.

I lead every session myself, not as a member who happens to have more experience, but as the strategist guiding the agenda toward what will actually move you forward. You need a room that gets it, and a facilitator who's spent 25+ years learning the difference between a growth problem and a strategy problem.
