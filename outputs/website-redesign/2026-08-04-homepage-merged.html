The Messy Middle

It Happens More Than Once

Most owners hit the first wall somewhere around $250,000–$400,000 when whatever got the business off the ground (hustle, doing everything yourself, figuring it out as you go) stops being enough. The second usually shows up around $750,000 to $1 million when the business gets too complex to hold in your head the way you used to. The third tends to hit around $3–4 million when the business has to shift from running on your judgment and presence to running as a professionally managed organization.

Each time it rarely feels like part of a pattern. It feels like something's wrong with the market, the team, or you. Usually it's none of those things. The strategy that worked to get you here needs an upgrade to get you to the next level.
