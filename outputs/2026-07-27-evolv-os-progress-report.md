## 1. Snapshot

Synnovatia is a solo strategic-consulting practice for bootstrapped B2B service businesses ($250K–$4M), delivering 1:1 consulting, monthly retainers, and two masterminds. The founder runs the business on ~3 hours a day while also carrying ~20 hours/week of graduate coursework — so recovered time is the entire point of the EVOLV-OS build.

In just over two weeks, the workspace has gone from an empty starter kit to **four installed layers** (Context, Data, Intelligence, plus the Automate layer now actively running), **~30 recurring business tasks inventoried**, and **23 of them systematized**. The system is not a demo — it is producing real business outcomes, including the practice's first re-engagement-driven client conversion.

**Definition used for Task Automation %:** a task counts as *systematized* when a scheduled task or a repeatable Claude-driven process now handles the mechanical work. Consistent with the EVOLV-OS "human-in-the-loop by default" principle, tasks where a human still reviews and sends (e.g. all client-facing email is draft-only) count as systematized — the drafting, tracking, and timing are offloaded; the judgment and the send stay human on purpose.

## 3. What's been built (the Automate layer)

### Data & Intelligence (runs daily, unattended)
- **Live business metrics** — Stripe revenue collected every morning at 6am; HubSpot CRM on demand. Feeds an auto-generated metrics file. *(Was: manual dashboard-checking.)*
- **The Dashboard** — a single business + personal snapshot (revenue vs. 2026/2027 goals, mastermind growth, health, checkable reminders) rebuilt and republished every morning at ~7am to a stable link. *(Was: ~5–10 min/day pulling three sources together.)*
- **Morning brief** — weekday 7:10am gather of calendar + unanswered email, rendered as a styled brief.
- **Meeting intelligence** — Zoom auto-records and auto-summarizes every meeting; any past meeting is searchable by name or topic. *(Was: ~10–15 min/meeting of manual notes.)*

### Client lifecycle (draft-only; human reviews & sends)
- **Client onboarding** — detects new Closed-Won deals, drafts the welcome email, tracks the agreement/profile/scheduling/invoice checklist, and nudges if anything stalls. *(Was: 20–30 min/client, manual.)*
- **Pre-meeting objective** and **post-meeting recap** — calendar-detected for 1:1 clients; drafts the objective ask 4 days out and a same-day recap with action items pulled from the Zoom summary.
- **Client re-engagement** — a 174-client roster on a 6-month cadence. Every Monday the system checks for replies, refreshes the due list, and drafts 5 personalized reconnection emails; the founder reviews and sends Tuesday. Includes a priority queue (pull warm contacts to the front) and reply/opportunity tracking. **This system produced the practice's first re-engagement conversion on 2026-07-22** — a client last active in 2011, reconnected via a cold email on July 6 and engaged for book coaching sixteen days later.

### Marketing (draft-only; runs on a content calendar)
- **LinkedIn** — 3 posts batch-drafted each Monday for the following week, in brand voice, with an automatic "AI-tell" cadence scrub before the founder sees them.
- **HubSpot email** — three audience segments (Active Engagers, Drifting, Lapsed) each on their own cadence, plus a new **send-performance log** that benchmarks every send's open/click/unsub rates over time.
- **Mastermind growth** — a reusable cohort-launch checklist, a twice-monthly candidate-pipeline review, and a systematic "roster mining" pass that surfaced 9 qualified prospects for the two groups.

### Operations & personal
- **Bookkeeping/bill-pay and account reconciliation** — reminders aligned to real bill-arrival dates, spanning the practice, household, and the founder's spouse's business. *(Money movement stays human by hard rule.)*
- **Home & health** — every recurring home-maintenance service (HVAC, pest, tree, granite, spa, garden, windows), the annual physical and eye exam, the weekly weigh-in, and the workout program are on automated reminders or logs.

## 6. What we learned building it (principles in practice)

- **Borrow before you build.** The re-engagement system was migrated from an existing build rather than re-created; the mastermind reminders reused one pattern across 20 dated tasks.
- **Human-in-the-loop by default.** Every client-facing and marketing email is draft-only. Money never moves without the founder. This is a design choice, not a limitation — it's why the system can be trusted to run unattended.
- **Reject fragile automation honestly.** Browser-automating LinkedIn posting and grocery-sale scanning were both tried and rejected as too unreliable; a paste-in with a reliable reminder beat them. The audit records these as deliberate "human by design," not gaps.
- **Verify before acting.** A pre-outreach research pass caught a former client with a **federal wire-fraud conviction** before a warm email went out — a reputational save the raw data would never have surfaced. Research now precedes outreach as a standing habit.
- **The data narrows; the human decides.** HubSpot's revenue field was wrong on every contact checked; retirements and business changes were invisible in every system. The tools shortlist; the founder's knowledge is the filter.
